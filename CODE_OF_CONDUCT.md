# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and direct. We welcome people with different experience levels, languages, identities, and streaming setups.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information, and hostile behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, releases, and related project spaces.

## Enforcement

If a conversation becomes harmful or unproductive, maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary. Report concerns privately to the repository owner.
